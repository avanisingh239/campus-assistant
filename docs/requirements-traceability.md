                 ┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
                 ▼                                             ▼

## 3. Unresolved Decisions & Open Assumptions

The following items are explicitly tracked as open product questions and **will not be implemented** until approved:
1. **[Unresolved Decision] Student Enrollment Verification:**  
   In MVP, students self-select department/section. Whether institutional email domains (`@university.edu`) or student ID verification will be required is deferred to Phase 2.
2. **[Unresolved Decision] Cross-Section Shared Course Cancellations:**  
   How to resolve when a course is shared across multiple sections, but only one section's CR posts a cancellation. (MVP assumes announcements are strictly section-scoped).
3. **[Unresolved Decision] Raw Message Data Retention Policy:**  
   A 30-day rolling purge of `raw_messages` was proposed, but **is NOT finalized**. Required future decision factors:
   * **Privacy & Data Minimization:** How long private student chat pastes should be held on the server.
   * **User Deletion Requests:** Mechanism for students to delete their uploaded raw messages immediately on demand.
   * **Debugging & Extraction Quality:** Retaining raw text long enough to diagnose AI parsing anomalies.
   * **Database Storage Limits:** PostgreSQL row and text storage capacity under large chat exports.
   * **Trace-to-Source Audit Availability:** Retaining raw messages as long as their derived announcements remain active and unarchived.

## 4. Confirmation of Non-Included Features

The following features were audited and are **CONFIRMED NOT INCLUDED**:
* ❌ **Open-Ended Conversational Chatbot:** Completely excluded. System is strictly an ingestion-to-structured-dashboard clarity pipeline.
* ❌ **Admin Moderation / Approval Queue:** Completely excluded. Admins submit canonical notices within their verified scope; they do not review student chat pastes.
* ❌ **Unapproved Calendar Export (.ics):** Excluded from MVP; tagged as an optional future proposal.
* ❌ **Unapproved Demo Scenario Switcher:** Excluded; replaced with the source-required Time-Decay simulation toggle.
* ❌ **Unapproved Haptic Feedback:** Excluded from MVP.
* ❌ **Extra Roles (Faculty, Dean, Superadmin):** Excluded. Strictly Student and Admin roles only.
